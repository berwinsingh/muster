Your API starts before your database is ready, and dies.

`concurrently`, `npm-run-all` and `turbo` can't fix that — they start
everything at once. Vercel closed the request for it as "not planned".

Muster waits for a ready signal, then starts what depends on it. One config,
one command, with or without VS Code.
